About Linda Covino

Experience designer, Certified Customer Experience Professional (CCXP) and founder of ExperienceDesignGuru. My story in seven chapters, from Caorle to Dubai.

Chapter one, Caorle: where it all started

My story starts by the sea in the Veneto, between Caorle and Portogruaro, where my family home is still today.

Hospitality starts with how you make people feel at home.

Chapter two, Milan: where I learned to see

Twenty years in Milan taught me to see everything as something designed. At Politecnico di Milano I completed a Master's in Product-Service System Design, and learned the idea I still work by: every experience can be designed on purpose.

Chapter three, Logotel: learning to listen

My first design home was Logotel, a service design company in Milan. Workshops, research, journeys and prototypes, always with real people on the other side.

Design with people, not just for them.

Chapter four, Deloitte Digital: thinking in systems

At Deloitte Digital I moved into customer experience strategy for large organisations. More brands, more channels, more moving parts.

A great idea only works when the whole organisation can deliver it.

Chapter five, Dubai: a new chapter in the Gulf

Five years ago I moved to Dubai. Today I am Experience Design and Innovation Director at Majid Al Futtaim, where I lead SHARE, the group's loyalty programme.

People rarely remember a transaction. They remember how you made them feel.

Chapter six, the traveller: 61 countries and counting

Travel is my research lab. Every check-in, tour and welcome kit is a small case study in experience design. I write about it on The Visualler.

Chapter seven, now: why ExperienceDesignGuru

I share what I have learned with hosts, hotels and travel brands: how to design experiences guests remember, and the stories that sell them. Explore the courses.
